# Owner actions for App Store submission
Status: ACTIONS REQUIRED. Detailed paths may vary with Apple UI.

1. Confirm legal operator name, public support email and privacy contact email. Confirm business/legal address if counsel advises it must appear in public terms. Approve policy/terms after reviewing live data practices.
2. App Store Connect > Apps > Apparently You > Monetization > Subscriptions: verify subscription group, monthly and annual products, pricing and availability; create an eligible introductory annual offer for first-year founders ONLY if Apple's offer mechanics support the desired targeting. If not, stop and decide a compliant alternative rather than promise an unsupported discount. Check renewal $29.99. Submit first subscriptions together with first app version.
3. RevenueCat dashboard > Project > Products / Entitlements / Offerings: verify matching Apple products, `apparently_private` entitlement, default offering and paywall packages. Test sandbox purchase, cancel, restore, renewal and founder eligibility.
4. App Store Connect > Apps > Apparently You > App Privacy: enter verified public Privacy Policy URL, complete each data-type disclosure for the app and SDKs, publish disclosures.
5. App Store Connect > App Information: complete category, age rating questionnaire, rights and other requested metadata. Private/Private Private content requires truthful answers, not an assumed rating.
6. App Store Connect > iOS app version 1.0: enter description, subtitle, keywords, support URL, marketing URL, copyright and screenshots from the actual final build. Choose Build 18 when available.
7. App Store Connect > App Review Information: provide real reviewer contact, notes and test access to premium areas. Verify live Supabase and RevenueCat work for reviewer.
8. App Store Connect > Business/Agreements, Tax and Banking: confirm all paid-app contracts, tax forms and banking details show active/completed status.
9. Test Build 18 on iPhone with monthly/annual/founder purchase scenarios, delete-account with and without active subscription, privacy/terms links, age gate and personality evidence >3 traits; keep screenshots/logs of results.
10. After all gates pass: app version > Add for Review > review draft submission including first subscriptions > Submit for Review. Select manual release if you want final control. Apple controls review timing.

Official guidance: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-app/
