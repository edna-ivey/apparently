## Four final premium reveals

### FIT: WAIT... YOU MIGHT ACTUALLY LIKE YOUR DREAM LIFE.

**THE VERDICT**

You planned a dream that might survive an average Tuesday. We're impressed.

**THE PRIVATE READ**

You seem to understand that a life isn't just a collection of achievements. It comes with responsibilities, routines, and ordinary mornings. Your answers suggest that what you want lines up reasonably well with how you actually like to live.

**THE CALL-OUT**

Knowing what fits you doesn't mean the blueprint can never change.

**THE COST**

You could become so attached to your original vision that you overlook a better life taking shape.

**TRY THIS**

Write down three parts of your dream that are essential to your happiness. Let the decorations stay negotiable.

**THE KICKER**

The house looks lovely. You even considered who's cleaning it.

*Apparently, your dream has a realistic floor plan.*

### LIFESTYLE: YOU WANT THE LIFE. JUST... NOT THE LIFESTYLE.

**THE VERDICT**

You ordered the highlight reel. Unfortunately, the package includes chores. 😭

**THE PRIVATE READ**

You aren't wrong for enjoying beautiful things, comfort, experiences, or convenience. But your answers suggest that the less glamorous work behind your dream hasn't received quite as much attention as the reward.

**THE CALL-OUT**

You may be shopping for a lifestyle by looking only at its best days.

**THE COST**

You could achieve exactly what you wanted and discover that maintaining it takes more of your life than enjoying it.

**TRY THIS**

Choose one dream. Calculate the weekly time, energy, money, and support required to maintain it. Outsourcing counts, as long as it's realistic.

**THE KICKER**

*Apparently, you want the penthouse. We need to discuss the plumbing.*

**YOUR TRAITS**

Dream-Oriented · Comfort-Loving · Selectively Practical

### RELIEF: YOU DON'T WANT A NEW LIFE. YOU WANT RELIEF.

**THE VERDICT**

Your dream keeps sounding suspiciously like nobody needs anything from you for a minute.

**THE PRIVATE READ**

Your answers suggest that what you're craving may be less about a particular house, city, job, or lifestyle and more about having room to breathe. You may have real responsibilities, real pressure, or too little support. Wanting relief is not a character flaw.

**THE CALL-OUT**

**THE COST**

Exhaustion can make everything feel wrong, including parts of your life you might still value under different conditions.

**TRY THIS**
