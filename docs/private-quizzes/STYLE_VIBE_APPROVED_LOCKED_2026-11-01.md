## Four final premium reveals

### APPROACHABLE: NOBODY IS SCARED OF YOU. 😂

**THE VERDICT**

We reviewed the allegations. The evidence suggests you're easier to approach than people claim.

**THE PRIVATE READ**

You tend to make room for conversation, acknowledge other people's effort, and handle disagreements without making every interaction a contest. That's a form of social confidence that doesn't require you to be the loudest person in the room.

**THE CALL-OUT**

Being approachable doesn't mean you need to absorb everyone's problems.

**THE COST**

People may assume you're always available because you're usually pleasant and receptive.

**TRY THIS**

Keep your warmth. Make your limits just as easy to understand.

**THE KICKER**

No intimidation charges. Several people would like to tell you their life story.

*Apparently, your vibe comes with an open-door policy.*

**YOUR TRAITS**

Approachable · Warm · Supportive

### FACE: IT'S THE FACE. THE FACE IS DOING A LOT.

**THE VERDICT**

Your expression is apparently sending emails you never approved. 😭

**THE PRIVATE READ**

People may read your neutral face, thoughtful silence, or straightforward delivery as disapproval. But the fact that someone feels intimidated doesn't automatically mean you're behaving aggressively.

**THE CALL-OUT**

You don't owe the world a permanent smile. Still, when an interaction matters to you, a small signal of warmth can prevent a big misunderstanding.

**THE COST**

Some people may decide you aren't interested in talking before you've actually had a conversation.

**TRY THIS**

When you welcome a conversation, make that clear with your words rather than relying on people to interpret your face.

**THE KICKER**

Your face has an entire public relations problem.

*Apparently, your expression has opinions you haven't reviewed.*

**YOUR TRAITS**

Reserved-Looking · Frequently Misread · Understated

### EVALUATIVE: YOU'RE NOT INTIMIDATING. YOU'RE HARD TO IMPRESS.

**THE VERDICT**

You don't need everybody to agree with you. You do need things to make sense.

**THE PRIVATE READ**

You notice details, ask follow-up questions, and prefer explanations that hold up under scrutiny. People sometimes experience that as pressure, even when your intention is to understand or improve something.

**THE CALL-OUT**

Your questions may be reasonable. Your timing and delivery still matter.

**THE COST**

People might stop sharing early ideas because they expect an immediate inspection.

**TRY THIS**

**THE KICKER**

Not everyone arrives with supporting documentation. Tragic.

*Apparently, your standards brought a clipboard.*

**YOUR TRAITS**

Observant · Analytical · High-Standards

### INTIMIDATING: OH. YOU ACTUALLY ARE INTIMIDATING.
